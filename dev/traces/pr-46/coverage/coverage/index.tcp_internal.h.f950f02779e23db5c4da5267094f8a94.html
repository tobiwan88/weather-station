1 /** @file
2 * @brief Transmission Control Protocol (TCP)
3 *
4 * - net_tcp_get() is called by net_context_get(NET_AF_INET, NET_SOCK_STREAM,
5 NET_IPPROTO_TCP, ...) and creates struct tcp for the net_context
6 * - net_tcp_listen()/net_tcp_accept() listen/accept
7 * - At the reception of SYN on the listening net_context, a new pair
8 * of net_context/struct tcp registers a new net_conn handle
9 * with the tcp_recv() as a callback
10 * - net_tcp_queue() queues the data for the transmission
11 * - The incoming data is delivered up through the context->recv_cb
12 * - net_tcp_put() closes the connection
13 *
14 * NOTE: The present API is provided in order to make the integration
15 * into the ip stack and the socket layer less intrusive.
16 *
17 * Semantically cleaner use is possible (and might be exposed),
18 * look into the unit test tests/net/tcp for insights.
