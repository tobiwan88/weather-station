2 /**
3 * @file fake_remote_sensor.c
4 * @brief Fake remote sensor transport — simulates wireless nodes on native_sim.
5 *
6 * Each "node" is identified by a 1-byte node_id (0, 1, 2, …) and a fixed
7 * 6-byte fake MAC address derived from the node_id. The transport:
8 *
9 * 1. Registers itself via REMOTE_TRANSPORT_DEFINE (STRUCT_SECTION_ITERABLE).
10 * 2. On scan_start: publishes REMOTE_DISCOVERY_FOUND events for all nodes.
11 * 3. On peer_add: starts a k_timer for that peer's auto-publish interval.
12 * 4. On send_trigger: immediately publishes one synthetic measurement.
13 * 5. Shell "fake_remote announce" — manually trigger discovery.
14 * 6. Shell "fake_remote publish" — manually trigger one measurement round.
15 *
16 * Synthetic values:
17 * temperature: 20.0 °C + node_id (to distinguish nodes)
18 * humidity: 50.0 %RH + node_id * 5
19 * co2: 800 ppm + node_id * 100
20 * voc: 25 IAQ + node_id * 50
21 */
