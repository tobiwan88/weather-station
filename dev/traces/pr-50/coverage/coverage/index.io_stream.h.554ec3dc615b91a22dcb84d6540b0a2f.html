2 /**
3 * @file io_stream.h
4 * @brief Generic I/O stream abstraction with pluggable backends.
5 *
6 * Provides a Unix-style vtable interface for sequential read/write operations
7 * with position tracking (seek, tell). Backends include flash (wrapping
8 * Zephyr's flash_img) and RAM buffer (for native_sim testing).
9 *
10 * All stream instances are statically allocated (no heap).
11 * Backend state is managed internally by each backend — consumers only
12 * interact with `struct io_stream` and the vtable wrapper functions.
13 *
14 * Constrained by ADR-017 (Generic I/O Stream Abstraction).
15 */
16
