14 /**
15 * The intent of LOCK_SCHED_SPINLOCK is to lock the scheduler's spinlock for
16 * the scope that follows (e.g. LOCK_SCHED_SPINLOCK { ... }). However, on
17 * single core systems, the scheduler's spinlock is not needed when the locked
18 * region would otherwise be encapsulated by another spinlock (as that spinlock
19 * would degenerate into an IRQ lock). For that degenerate case, this macro
20 * will expand to nothing.
21 */

24 #else
